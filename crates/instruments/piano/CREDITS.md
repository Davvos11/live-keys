# Grand Piano credits

live-keys' Grand Piano plays the **Salamander Grand Piano V3** samples by **Alexander Holm**
(a Yamaha C5), through an SFZ adapted from the one in
[sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano)
(retuned version by Markus Fiedler, SFZ reconstructed by kinwie).

Both are licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

Changes to the SFZ (`salamander.sfz`, compiled into live-keys): the key-release noises sound
when the key comes up (`trigger=release_key`), and controllers were added for brightness,
tuning and stereo width. The samples themselves are downloaded separately, unchanged.
